#### Staff 原回答

Shailendra Reddy（Staff）；[原文定位](https://edstem.org/au/courses/36662/discussion/3462313?comment=7570073)；2026-07-28 13:08（奥克兰本地时间）。

> Relevance of company selection and problem domain selection.
>
> To begin with, we will have four problems per organisation. One problem in each domain (Strategic/Competitive, Operational, Organisational, or Financial)
>
> These problems will be worked on through A1 (D1 and D2) and A2 (D1 and D2) - individually.
>
> When we come to A3 - The team members will discuss the 4 problems and their solutions, weigh their pros and cons, and use a decision-making framework to prioritise their implementation, and choose the highest priority solution, one of the four to implement (Design and Build App Prototype using SAP Build Code).
>
> This is a real, practical IT consultancy scenario where all consultants bring their thoroughly developed, great solutions to the table to solve pressing problems, but for various reasons (which students will experience), only a few are selected and prioritised for implementation. Other solutions are shelved or put on the back burner, and all join hands to accept and deliver on the decision.
>
> A4 is an individual assignment; its details will be discussed later in the course.
