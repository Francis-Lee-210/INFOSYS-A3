# Visual and delivery system

- Use 16:9, one declarative headline, one principal visual and no more than three short support labels per slide.
- Use the full slide width, reserving only a 304 × 171 px, 16:9 camera area at the lower right on the 1280 × 720 design canvas. Keep important content outside that corner and place page numbers at the lower left. Do not reserve a full-height right column.
- Use approximately 32 pt minimum audience text and 54–72 pt numerical headlines. Place compact source strips only on evidence/framework slides.
- Prefer editable vectors, charts and process strips; do not paste A2-D1 tables or full BPMN models into the video.
- Use a calm navy/white system with teal for evidence/release, amber for uncertainty and red only for stop/risk/negative economics. Do not use Spark proprietary assets without permission.
